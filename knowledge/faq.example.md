# FAQ example

Copy to `faq.md` (without `.example`) and edit. Each `##` heading is a question.

## What is Funke?

Funke is a self-hosted personal assistant for calendar, reminders, and social drafts. Nothing posts until you approve the exact final text.

## How do I approve a post?

Open the approval card in chat or the Ok tab. Review the final text, then tap Approve. Voice cannot approve.
